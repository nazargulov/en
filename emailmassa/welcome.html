Start your first Gmail campaign.

EmailMassa appears inside Gmail. Follow these steps to connect your account and send a safe test before starting a real campaign.

  1. 1. Open Gmail. Reload an existing Gmail tab after installation.
  2. 2. Connect Google. Click the EmailMassa icon and authorize the permissions used for sending and selected Google Sheets.
  3. 3. Start a compose window. Click the arrow beside the EmailMassa send button to open campaign options.
  4. 4. Import recipients. Select a Sheet, choose a CSV file, or paste a list.
  5. 5. Preview and test. Verify personalization and send a test message to yourself.
  6. 6. Send or schedule. Start immediately or choose a future date and time.
Send only to recipients who expect your email. Follow applicable anti-spam laws and Gmail policies.
